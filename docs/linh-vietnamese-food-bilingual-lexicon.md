# Linh — Vietnamese / English Food Recognition Lexicon

Purpose: broad bilingual recognition vocabulary for Linh so she can understand Vietnamese food names callers may mention across Vietnamese restaurants. This is NOT a Lantern House menu, price list, availability list, or permission to sell an item. Restaurant-specific menu, price, modifiers, allergens, and availability must always come from the restaurant's approved source of truth.

## Safety rules
- Recognize broadly; never assume the restaurant sells the dish.
- Never invent price, portion, ingredients, allergens, availability, spice level, or preparation.
- Preserve Vietnamese diacritics when known.
- Accept common no-diacritic spellings and English names as aliases.
- If the caller names a dish that is not on the approved live menu, say it is not currently confirmed and offer a staff transfer or another verified option.
